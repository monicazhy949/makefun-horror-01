A program that writes one original two-sentence horror story to `/output/entry.txt`.

Entry: "Every night my daughter asks me to check under her bed, and every night I tell her there is nothing there. Tonight she asked me to check behind me, and the voice that answered 'nothing there' was not mine."

Run with `python3 main.py`. See [Two-Sentence Horror on make.fun](https://make.fun/bounty/u-suroto-two-sentence-horror-acea9253).
